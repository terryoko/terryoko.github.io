Evaluating Prompting Strategies for African Languages in Low-Resource NLP
Anuj Tiwari, Oluwapelumi Ogunremu, Terry Oko-odion, Hannah Nwokocha, Jesujuwon Egbewale
Under review at the LoResLM Workshop (EACL 2026)
Systematically evaluates prompt engineering strategies, including instruction language, few-shot scaling, and cultural framing, across diverse African languages and tasks. The study distinguishes between model limitations and prompt design issues, providing evidence-based guidelines for optimizing performance in low-resource classification settings.

Sample-Size Scaling of the African Languages NLI Evaluation
Anuj Tiwari, Oluwapelumi Ogunremu, Terry Oko-odion, Hannah Nwokocha, Jesujuwon Egbewale
Under review at the AfricaNLP Workshop (EACL 2026)
Conducts a systematic scaling study on 16 African languages using AfriXNLI, revealing that small evaluation sets yield unstable, optimistically biased results. The work establishes that scaling behavior is non-monotonic and language-specific, emphasizing the critical need for larger, representative evaluation sets to ensure reliable benchmarking.
